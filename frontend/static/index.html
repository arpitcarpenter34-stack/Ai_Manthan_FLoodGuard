Urban Topography

Engineered for Indore's 45m Gravity Slope

Indore benefits from a natural South-to-North elevation drop from the southern Rau ridge (560m MSL) down to the northern Kabithedi terminal (515m MSL). FloodGuard AI coordinates municipal sluice gates and pumps to optimize this natural gravity drainage.

⛰️

560m Rau Ridge

Southern headwaters capturing high-intensity monsoon runoff into the Saraswati river feeder.

🏛️

540m Rajwada Confluence

Historic Krishnapura Chhatri junction where Saraswati and Khan rivers converge into a main conduit.

🏭

515m Kabithedi STP

245 MLD terminal treatment facility with 6 high-lift pumps discharging to the northern river outfall.
